About this calendar

This is the Assamese Calendar 2026 (অসমীয়া কেলেণ্ডাৰ ২০২৬), showing all twelve months with their corresponding Assamese (Saka) dates and Tithi (lunar day) information sourced from the traditional Assamese Panjika.


The Assamese calendar is a solar sidereal calendar used in Assam, India. Each Gregorian month displays the corresponding Assamese month names in the Saka era (শক সম্বৎ). The year 2026 CE spans Saka years ১৪৩২–১৪৩৩.
